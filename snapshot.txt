[16 Mar 2023] CORENET X Leadership Forum

Jointly organised with Singapore Polytechnic, CORENET X had our inaugural leadership forum on 16 March 2023. The forum was graced by Senior Minister of State Tan Kiat How, who gave an opening address. He shared about how leaders play a crucial role in driving transformation by setting the direction and culture within the organisations as well as investing in one of the most valuable assets - our people, to equip them with the necessary sklillsets and training, to build a more competitive BE workforce. He encouraged leaders to rally their teams to start preparing, to be ready ahead of the launch. You may view his full speech here.

The forum is supported by SIA, ACES, BOA and PEB, and will complement more detailed existing and future CX industry training. The forum also saw IFC+SG training providers - AcePLP, BIMAGE and Singapore Polytechnic who had set up booths to share their knowledge and expertise.

The forum consisted of 3 presentation topics, including a sharing by an industry leader - Ar. Lin Hongsui from ID Architects Pte Ltd.

1

Preparing for Change: Navigating a New Process
by Er. Clement Tseng, Group Director, BCA

Understand the impetus for change and how the new regulatory approval processes serve to enhance productivity through better collaboration and coordination.

Download Slides: Presentation 1 by Er. Clement Tseng (BCA)

 2

 Being Future-Ready: Embracing a New Model
by Ar. Tan Jwu Yih, Director, BCA

Understand the importance of data standardisation and how firms can benefit and build new capabilities.

Download Slides: Presentation 2 by Ar. Tan Jwu Yih (BCA)

 3	 Leading the Way: IDA's Insights from their Digital Transformation Journey towards CORENET X
by Ar. Lin Hongsui, Director, ID Architects Pte Ltd

An industry perspective on the benefits, challenges, and the necessary in-house preparation.

Download Slides: Presentation 3 by Ar. Lin Hongsui (IDA)
