[2026-09-01] ENHANCED TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS IN CODE OF PRACTICE FOR RAILWAY PROTECTIONFOR DEVELOPMENT WORKS AFFECTING BORED TUNNELS IN COMPRESSIBLE SOILS
Agencies: LTA
Categories: Requirements
Proposed works in the vicinity of compressible soils can have significant impact on RTS tunnels as compared to other type of soils. To manage the risks posed by such works, LTA will be revising the relevant technical requirements in Code of Practice for Railway Protection (CPRP).  The enhanced Code requires consolidation analysis to be carried out to assess long-term ground changes and impact to existing RTS tunnels for development and building works near railway tunnels in compressible soils. Qualified Persons (QPs) are also required to minimize additional loading and groundwater drawdown and demonstrate that there is no adverse impact to railway tunnels in compressible soils.

Agencies: NEA

The Registered Noise Consultants Scheme (RNCS) was introduced in April 2019 to expedite the processing time for Air-conditioning and Mechanical Ventilation (ACMV) noise assessment applications submitted by registered noise consultants.  The scheme's success relies on engaging qualified and competent industry practitioners who possess technical proficiency in acoustics, thorough knowledge of technical guidelines, and expertise in the appropriate use of sound level measuring equipment. Since its implementation, more than 800 noise assessment reports had been submitted by registered noise consultants. This review seeks to enhance the scheme's competitiveness and accessibility for acoustic professionals whilst ensuring Qualified Persons (QPs) and developers have access to an active pool of registered consultants.  The review concentrated on two key aspects: (i) establishing requirements for existing registered noise consultants to maintain an active RNC listing and (ii) modifying entry requirements for new applicants to create a more effective and inclusive scheme.
